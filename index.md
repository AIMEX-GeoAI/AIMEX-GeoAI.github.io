# AIMEX Lab
**Artificial Intelligence for Mineral Exploration Laboratory**

AIMEX Lab develops interpretable, data-driven, and reproducible AI workflows for geochemistry, ore-system science, spatial geoscience, and mineral exploration.

**Founded and directed by Dr. Muhammad Amar Gul**

AIMEX focuses on transparent scientific machine learning, geochemical big-data analytics, reproducible validation, and practical mineral-exploration decision support.

## Research themes

- GeoAI and interpretable machine learning for mineral exploration
- Mineral and whole-rock geochemistry
- Metallogenic discrimination and ore-system science
- Remote sensing and spatial geoscience
- Reproducible benchmarking and validation
