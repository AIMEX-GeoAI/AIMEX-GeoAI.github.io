<section class="section-shell">
  <p class="section-kicker">Research workflow</p>
  <h2 class="section-title">Evidence before prediction.</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">AIMEX workflows are designed to preserve geological meaning from source data through model evaluation and interpretation.</p>
  <div class="workflow">
    <div class="step"><b>1. Data authority</b><p>Define provenance, scope, exclusions, and QA/QC before modeling.</p></div>
    <div class="step"><b>2. Geological representation</b><p>Engineer variables without discarding mineral-system or spatial context.</p></div>
    <div class="step"><b>3. Model & validation</b><p>Use fit-for-purpose algorithms, leakage controls, and robust holdout logic.</p></div>
    <div class="step"><b>4. Scientific interpretation</b><p>Separate predictive performance from geological inference and decision support.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>
