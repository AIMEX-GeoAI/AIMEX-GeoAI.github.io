# About AIMEX Lab

AIMEX Lab is a research and innovation initiative founded and directed by **Dr. Muhammad Amar Gul**, focused on artificial intelligence for mineral exploration and geoscience.

## Mission

Develop transparent, scientifically defensible AI workflows that integrate geochemistry, spatial geoscience, remote sensing, mineral-system knowledge, and reproducible validation for exploration research and decision support.

## Vision

Build a collaborative GeoAI research platform that advances mineral-exploration science while preserving geological meaning, uncertainty, reproducibility, and independent validation.

## Research principles

- Scientific validity before model performance
- Interpretable and auditable workflows
- Reproducible data processing and validation
- Geological and spatial context preserved throughout analysis
- Explicit separation of research evidence, inference, uncertainty, and hypothesis
- Prospective and external validation where feasible

## Founder & Director

**Dr. Muhammad Amar Gul** is a geoscientist and GeoAI researcher working across mineral geochemistry, economic geology, machine learning, spatial data science, and mineral exploration.
